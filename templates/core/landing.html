AI-Powered Audio Summarization

AudioBrief uses two open-source AI models, OpenAI Whisper and Facebook BART, to turn spoken audio into a written transcript and a short summary. It is handy for lectures, meetings, interviews and podcasts: upload a file, follow the progress live, and download the result as a PDF.

Experience Audio in a New Way

Whisper turns speech into text, and BART condenses long transcripts into short, readable summaries.

  • Speech-to-text with OpenAI Whisper
  • Abstractive summaries with BART
  • Live progress while your file is processed

How It Works

Convert an audio file to text and a concise summary in three simple steps

1

Upload Audio

Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or AAC file (up to 100 MB).

2

AI Processing

Whisper transcribes the speech and BART summarizes the text. You can watch each stage live.

3

Get Summary

Receive your transcript and concise summary, highlighting the most important points.

Powerful Features

AudioBrief turns audio files into transcripts and summaries you can read in minutes.

Audio Transcription

Convert English-language audio recordings into text with OpenAI Whisper.

Smart Summaries

Get a short summary of the key points, even for long recordings, generated by Facebook's BART model.

YouTube Summaries Coming soon

A planned future enhancement: paste a YouTube link and get a summary. Not available yet.

Live Progress

Follow every stage (upload, preparing, transcribing, summarizing) on a real progress bar, and cancel any time.

Common Formats

Works with MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and AAC files, converted automatically with ffmpeg.

PDF Export

Download the transcript and summary as a neat PDF to save, print or share.
